## Brand & Style

The design system is rooted in the principles of Swiss Modernism—precision, legibility, and objectivity. It is tailored for a premium construction and waterproofing firm in Zurich (Fugenmeister GmbH), where the UI must mirror the physical durability and engineering excellence of the services provided.

The visual narrative is built on **Modern Corporate Minimalism**. It utilizes a structured grid, generous whitespace, and a high-contrast palette to evoke a sense of unshakable reliability.
